## Refines no longer paste the model's commentary

A refine sometimes replaced your text with the rewrite **and** an explanation
of it:

> I'm not sure. It seems they are quite busy, but this probably needs to be
> discussed with Bryan.
>
> **What changed:** The original had a comma splice joining ...

All of that went into your message box. It was worse with a custom `/rd`
instruction or a friendly preset — because those replace the default direction
entirely, and the only wording telling the model to reply with just the text
lived inside that default. So the cases most likely to draw commentary were
running with nothing asking for restraint.

redrafter now asks for the rewrite in a delimiter it can read back, adds that
request to every refine regardless of your direction or preset, and trims the
usual commentary shapes when a model ignores it: trailing "What changed:" or
"Note:" blocks, "Here's the corrected text:" preambles, code fences wrapped
around the whole reply, and quotes around the entire rewrite.

The trimming is deliberately cautious — losing a line you wanted is worse than
keeping one you didn't. A message that legitimately starts "Note: bring your
passport", one that quotes a code block, and quotation marks that are part of
your sentence are all left alone.
